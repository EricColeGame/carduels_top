* **Brake Checking:** When an enemy lines up a ram from behind, applying a sudden brake or counter-steer forces them to clip your reinforced bumper at an angle, redirecting their force outward.
* **Platform Awareness:** Arena edges often have varying friction or dynamic hazards. Keep your camera angled downward to monitor wheel distance from boundary lines.
