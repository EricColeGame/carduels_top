## Getting Started: Mechanics, Controls, and Game Modes

At its core, the game revolves around team-based sumo mechanics. Two factions, Red and Blue, line up across an elevated combat arena. The goal is deceptively simple: slam opposing vehicles off the rim while keeping your own four wheels firmly on the deck. Matches reward the surviving team with Cash, the primary currency used to upgrade chassis resistance and offensive bumper kits.

To compete effectively, players across all platforms must master precision input. Unlike standard street racing titles, sudden oversteering in an arena setting leaves your rear exposed to devastating T-bone impacts.

### The Three Phases of an Arena Match

1. **The Opening Break:** Never lead the front line unless you are running a maximum-weight build. Let over-aggressive enemies miss their initial rush, oversteer near the periphery, and slide into vulnerable positions.
2. **Center Control:** Control the inner ring. By holding the center of the arena floor, any knockback you absorb merely pushes you toward the outer ring rather than immediately over the cliff.
3. **Edge Trapping and Sweeping:** When enemies drift near the platform borders to recover boost, hit them at a 45-degree angle. Angular collisions bypass frontal bumper armor and induce uncontrollable spin-outs.

| Tactical Approach | Execution Method | Best Used Against | Primary Risk |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **T-Bone Ambush** | Strike rivals engaged in existing duels | Distracted or stalled opponents | Exposing your own flank to outside hits |
| **Reverse Push** | Back up against low bumpers to hook edges | Lightweight speedsters | Slower reverse acceleration speeds |

By rotating between these maneuvers, you dictate the pace of every encounter rather than relying on chaotic physics coin-flips. Always consult our **carduels guide** strategy framework whenever you encounter coordinated squads running dual-heavy setups.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the most effective stat to level up first?
Chassis Curb Weight should always be your initial investment. Increasing weight reduces the distance your vehicle is launched when struck by aggressive opponents. Once your car can reliably anchor itself in the center ring, allocate your Cash into bumper impact force.

### How do promo codes work in the game?
Players can redeem codes directly through the in-game Shop or Codes menu. Developers periodically publish codes to celebrate visit milestones, updates, and community events. Redeeming these codes awards instant Cash bonuses that bypass early game grinding.

### Can lightweight cars beat heavy chassis builds?
Yes. While heavy vehicles dominate stationary pushing matches, lightweight cars boast superior acceleration and handling. By baiting heavy builds toward the arena rim and swerving out of the way at the final moment, agile drivers can use an opponent's high momentum against them.

### What should I focus on in this carduels guide if I play solo?
Solo players should focus primarily on center-ring positioning and edge-baiting tactics. In uncoordinated public lobbies, solo players often get caught in crossfire, so maintaining high chassis weight and holding the center platform provides the highest consistent survival rate.
