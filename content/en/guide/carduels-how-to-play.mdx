## Core Rules and Objective: Understanding the Vehicle Sumo Arena

At its heart, Car DUELS is a team-based vehicle sumo competition. Two sides—the Red Team and the Blue Team—spawn on opposite ends of a high platform. Your primary objective is deceptively simple: ram opposing vehicles over the boundary ledge while keeping your own four wheels securely on the asphalt.

Matches continue until one team's vehicles have all been knocked into the void. Surviving rounds and contributing knockouts rewards you with Cash, the core in-game currency used to purchase advanced vehicles, reinforced frames, and heavier bumpers.

### Mastering the Bumper Angle
Direct head-on collisions usually favor the car with higher mass and a sturdier bumper tier. However, if you clip an opponent's rear quarter-panel at a 45-degree angle, you bypass their frontal defenses. This destabilizes their steering, rotating their vehicle toward the drop zone while your forward momentum stays firmly grounded.

## Advanced Combat Tactics to Dominate Red vs. Blue

Once you understand the controls and garage upgrades, knowing **carduels how to play** at an advanced tier comes down to tactical decision-making in the arena.

### The Anchor Strategy
Never circle the outer rim of the arena unless you are intentionally luring an aggressive rookie. Keep your wheels firmly rotating around the center platform zone. Controlling the center means you have 360 degrees of recovery space if struck. If an opponent hits you, friction and distance will slow your slide before you tumble into the abyss.

### The Pit-Maneuver Shove
Avoid straight frontal hits when battling equivalent weight classes. Instead, bait the opponent into accelerating forward, use a small burst of reverse or handbrake to dodge their nose, and immediately ram their rear tire. This sends their vehicle into an uncontrollable spin toward the boundary line.

### Safe Cash Farming Strategy
Winning rounds yields the best payouts, but survival time also matters. If your team is down several cars, switch entirely to defensive maneuvering. Dodge incoming charges, let remaining rivals make reckless mistakes near edges, and secure round-end placement bonuses to maximize your earnings per hour.
