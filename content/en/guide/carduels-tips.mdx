## Core Mechanics and Collision Physics

At its foundation, Car DUELS is a vehicle sumo experience. Success is not just about raw top speed; it revolves around momentum, center of mass, and angle of attack. Knowing how weight values interact with bumper force allows you to initiate collisions you can reliably win.

When two vehicles collide head-on, the car with higher vehicle mass and superior bumper momentum retains forward velocity, transferring kinetic impact to the lighter opponent. Hitting an adversary at an angle—especially against their rear quarter panels—bypasses front bumper defenses entirely, spinning them outward toward the hazard perimeter.

### Collision Priority Matrix

| Encounter Type | Recommended Player Action | Physics Outcome | Risk Level |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Head-on Clash** | Apply forward boost at the final second | The heavier bumper wins; deflects lighter car backward | High |
| **Flank / T-Bone** | Target enemy midsection while they turn | Heavy lateral shove; destabilizes enemy steering | Low |
| **Rear Shove** | Pursue fleeing enemies toward the edge | Propels the opponent directly off the arena ledge | Minimal |
| **Glancing Blow** | Veer away to clip bumper edge | Spins both cars; resets neutral positioning | Medium |

Timing your acceleration is crucial. Drivers who engage their speed boost too early often overshoot their target or leave themselves vulnerable to counter-steering. Conserve your burst until impact is inevitable to maximize pushing leverage.

## Arena Positioning and Edge Survival

Surviving on elevated platforms demands strict spatial awareness. Staying near the outer border exposes your ride to instant knockouts from surprise angles, while controlling the arena center provides defensive security.

```
Arena Center (Safe Zone) ---> Middle Ring (Engagement Zone) ---> Platform Edge (Danger Zone)
```

1. **Dominate the Center Ground:** Maintain your position in the central third of the arena floor whenever possible.
2. **Bait Overzealous Rams:** When pushed toward an edge, do not reverse directly back. Instead, brake softly and cut your steering sharply sideways, allowing aggressive pursuers to fly off the platform under their own momentum.
3. **Monitor Platform Hazards:** Certain arenas feature shifting boundaries or slippery terrain. Adjust your steering sensitivity to avoid sliding past edges during fast turns.

### Arena Positioning Strategy Table

| Position Zone | Tactical Advantages | Major Hazards | Optimal Tactic |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Central Hub** | Maximum recovery distance from all edges | Susceptible to converging strikes from multiple foes | Hold ground, pivot smoothly, punish lunges |
| **Mid Ring** | Excellent angles for flanking enemy lines | Moderate exposure to chain-reaction shoves | Circle outer brawls and pick off stray cars |
| **Platform Ledge** | Can lure opponents into fatal overshoots | Extreme risk of instant ring-out elimination | Feint recovery, sideslip, counter-shove |

Implementing these spatial **carduels tips** will keep your vehicle grounded long after aggressive, uncalculated opponents have thrown themselves off the map.
