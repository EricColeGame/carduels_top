## Tactical Arena Sumo: Winning Trades to Multiply Earnings

Every arena elimination provides instant cash, but recklessly charging opponents often results in you flying off the platform instead. Understanding sumo momentum physics is crucial. In Car DUELS, momentum, vehicle weight, and point of impact determine who flies backward.

If you hit an opponent head-on while both are boosting, the vehicle with higher mass and superior bumper stats wins the exchange. However, an angled hit against the side or rear wheels of an opponent will send them spinning out regardless of their size, granting you an easy ring-out and the associated cash bounty.

| Combat Situation | Recommended Action | Outcome | Income Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Direct Head-on Charge** | Brake slightly, steer offset, boost into their rear quarter | Enemy loses traction and slides toward edge | High (Quick ring-out reward) |
| **Opponent Camping Platform Edge** | Circle back to center, force them to drive inward | Avoids suicidal double-ring-out | Protects survival streak bonus |
| **Crowded Multi-Car Clash** | Hang back near mid-ring, ram external cluster | Multi-knockout opportunity with minimal risk | Massive payout burst |
| **Stuck Near Boundary** | Reverse immediately, do not boost forward blindly | Resets positioning toward high-ground center | Prevents losing match reward multipliers |

### Essential Driving Tactics for High-Reward Rounds

- **Control Center Ice:** Never linger within two vehicle lengths of the arena boundary. Controlling the geometric center forces opponents to take long, risky boost angles to challenge you.
- **Feint and Counter-Boost:** Accelerate toward an incoming enemy, tap your brakes to let them whiff their boost charge, then turbo into their exposed rear chassis as they slide past.
- **Bumper Alignment:** Make sure your impact registration connects squarely with their wheels. Off-center wheel hits disrupt vehicle physics engines faster than flat door panels.

Implementing disciplined driving lines ensures you rarely drop rounds, keeping your winning cash multipliers active match after match.
