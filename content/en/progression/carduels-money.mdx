### The "Bumper First" Philosophy
Player experience confirms that investing early **carduels money** into bumper upgrades yields the fastest return on investment. A top-tier bumper allows you to secure ring-outs even against heavier opponents if you catch them off-balance or strike their rear quarter-panels. Increased eliminations directly translate into higher match payouts, creating a positive feedback loop for your bankroll.

### Weight as an Insurance Policy
Once your bumper can launch light cars, transition your budget toward chassis reinforcement. In chaotic 8-player scrums, side impacts are inevitable. A lightweight car with a massive bumper will still fly into the void if it takes a blind hit from the side. Increasing mass ensures that even if you take a bad trade near the ledge, your tires stay planted on the tarmac.

Never stay near the outer rim of the arena unless you are intentionally baiting an opponent into an overextension. Experienced drivers utilize "matador" tactics: positioning near the ledge, waiting for an aggressive opponent to boost forward, and swerving at the last second. When executed cleanly, the attacker's own momentum sends them flying off the stage, earning you an easy elimination payout without risking bumper wear.
