## Stat Priorities: Weight vs. Bumper Force

Raw horsepower alone will not guarantee a win in arena sumo. The combat engine heavily weights impact momentum, calculated by your vehicle’s total mass combined with the multiplier of your equipped bumper.

```
Total Impact = (Vehicle Mass + Bumper Mass) x Collision Velocity x Force Multiplier
```

1. **Bumper Impact Force**: Directly amplifies the impulse imparted on an opponent upon contact.

## Arena Combat Tactics for New Vehicles

Piloting your newly acquired vehicles effectively requires mastering drift mechanics, timing your boost charges, and capitalizing on opponent errors.

```
       [Arena Perimeter (High Danger)]
                   |
     [Mid Ring: Staging & Baiting]
                   |
```

### Edge Recovery and Counter-Ramming

The perimeter represents the highest danger zone in Car DUELS. If pushed toward the drop-off:

1. **Do not slam the reverse gear immediately**: Abrupt reversing often destabilizes your rear tires, causing you to slip over the rim.
2. **Cut wheels perpendicular to the edge**: Slide parallel to the lip to bleed off knockback energy.
3. **Trigger boost back toward the center**: Accelerate into open asphalt while the opponent is still recovering from their forward momentum.

| Tactic | Execution | Risk Level | Reward |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **The Bull Rush** | Straight-line maximum boost directly into opponent | High | Instant ring-out against light targets |
| **The Edge Bait** | Idle near perimeter, jink sideways as enemy charges | Moderate | Free elimination via opponent overshooting |
| **The Anchor Hold**| Plant a heavy chassis dead-center and absorb hits | Low | High control, steady attrition wins |

When testing out **carduels new cars**, spend a few rounds in low-stakes lobbies to gauge their center of gravity and brake response before challenging top-tier drivers.
