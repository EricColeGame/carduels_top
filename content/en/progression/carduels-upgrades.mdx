## Core Garage Mechanics: How Upgrades Function

Every duel inside the arena revolves around physics-based collision trading. When two vehicles collide head-on, momentum, bumper force, and chassis weight determine which car yields ground. Spending Cash inside the Garage lets you modify these foundational attributes.

Before spending any currency, drivers must understand how mechanical stats interact during a crash. Increasing your acceleration without sufficient mass will only cause you to bounce backward upon impact. Conversely, loading up on raw weight without adequate bumper force creates a sluggish target that opponents can bully toward the ledge.

| Stat Type | Primary Benefit | Sumo Arena Impact | Priority Level |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Bumper Force** | Increases outgoing knockback power | Decisive factor in launching rivals over edges | **S-Tier** |
| **Vehicle Weight** | Increases knockback resistance | Prevents your car from being pushed backward | **S-Tier** |
| **Acceleration / Boost** | Reaches top speed quickly | Helps recover from arena edges and close gaps | **A-Tier** |
| **Handling / Grip** | Improves turning radius and traction | Prevents skidding off slippery platform borders | **B-Tier** |
| **Top Speed** | High sustained velocity | Useful for large arenas, negligible on small platforms | **C-Tier** |

Focusing early resources on bumper strength and mass creates immediate stability. While cosmetic parts look stylish, performance components directly dictate whether your front-end trades result in clean ring-outs or embarrassing knockouts.

## Comparing Bumper Styles and Impact Ratings

Bumpers serve as your main offensive weapon in Car DUELS. Equipping the wrong style can undermine your car's natural mass. Through extensive player experience, the community has cataloged how specific bumper designs alter momentum vectors during high-speed clashes.

Flat, heavy bumpers deliver horizontal force, making them ideal for shoving enemies directly back. Angled or wedge designs redirect momentum, popping rivals into the air where they lose tire traction completely.

| Bumper Classification | Knockback Direction | Ideal Playstyle | Strengths | Weaknesses |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Heavy Ram Bar** | Pure Horizontal | Aggressive Bulldozer | Exceptional raw power; dominates head-on clashes | High weight penalty on initial acceleration |
| **Wedge Plow** | Upward / Diagonal | Counter-Puncher | Flips opponents, negating their steering | Requires precise head-on targeting |
| **Reinforced Spikes** | Scattered Lateral | Flanker / Edge Skirmer | Strong glancing blows; forces opponents sideways | Weaker against max-weight tank builds |
| **Standard Stock** | Balanced Low | Beginner | Low Cash cost; no handling penalties | Easily overwhelmed by any upgraded bumper |

Selecting your bumper type should complement your overall chassis setup. If you run a lighter, agile vehicle, wedge-style bumpers let you leverage speed into vertical lift, overcoming the opponent's raw weight advantage.

## Weight Classes and Build Synergies

Maximizing the effectiveness of your vehicle means aligning your modifications with your chassis category. A heavy frame handles collisions differently than an agile compact. Evaluating how your **carduels upgrades** modify these archetypes prevents you from fighting against your vehicle's inherent strengths.

Below is an overview of the three primary garage archetypes, detailing how to invest your Cash for maximum competitive synergy.

| Vehicle Archetype | Core Stat Focus | Recommended Bumper | Primary Role | Countered By |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Heavyweight Juggernaut** | Maximum Weight, Raw Bumper Force | Heavy Ram Bar | Center control, shoving groups | Wedge flippers, agile flanking cars |
| **Midweight Striker** | Balanced Weight, High Boost | Wedge Plow | Pick-offs, edge punishing | Coordinated multi-car team shoves |
| **Lightweight Interceptor** | Acceleration, Maximum Grip | Reinforced Spikes | Flanking, disrupting, hit-and-run | Direct head-on ramming attacks |

### Tuning the Heavyweight Juggernaut
For heavy builds, channel every dollar into reinforcement plating and bumper mass. Once you achieve high resistance, opponents attempting to ram you head-on will rebound backward off your chassis, often eliminating themselves.

### Tuning the Midweight Striker
Midweights rely on burst speed to deliver knockout blows. Prioritize acceleration alongside wedge bumpers. Your goal is to intercept distracted heavyweights from the flank, using their own momentum to launch them off the perimeter.

### Tuning the Lightweight Interceptor
Lightweight setups require high mechanical discipline. Focus heavily on grip and recovery boosters. Since head-on trades are dangerous, you need top-tier traction to out-maneuver opponents, clip their rear quarter panels, and safely brake before running out of asphalt.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the best carduels upgrades to buy first?
The best initial investments are bumper force and chassis weight. These two stats directly govern how hard you hit opponents and how well you resist incoming shoves. Boosting your offensive knockback early ensures you secure more ring-outs, generating the Cash needed for handling and speed upgrades later.

### Does vehicle weight reduce your top speed?
Yes, increasing vehicle weight slightly lowers overall acceleration and agility. However, in an arena-based sumo mode, mass is vastly more valuable than top speed. Surviving a multi-car collision at the center of the ring matters far more than reaching extreme speeds along the dangerous outer rim.

### How often should I re-tune my setup for arena matches?
You should adjust your tuning whenever you transition between solo queue matches and team-based Red vs Blue battles. In team lobbies, high-mass defensive builds excel at holding the line, whereas smaller lobbies reward agile cars equipped with wedge bumpers capable of isolating single targets.
