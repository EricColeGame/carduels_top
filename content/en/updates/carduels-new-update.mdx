### Edge Juking vs. Center Holding

The new patch has created two primary schools of competitive strategy:

- **The Matador Tactic (Edge Juking):** Approach the platform boundary deliberately to entice aggressive rivals into charging. At the final fraction of a second, apply sharp counter-steering while tapping reverse. When timed correctly, the attacker’s forward momentum carries them completely off the edge.
- **The Pillar Tactic (Center Holding):** Utilize a medium or heavy chassis to occupy the absolute center of the platform. By rotating on an axis rather than driving long laps, you minimize the risk of overshooting while forcing opponents to fight against your weight advantages.
