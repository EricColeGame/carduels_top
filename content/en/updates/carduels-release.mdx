## Mastering the Duel Mechanic: How Battles Work

At the heart of the platform sits the duel mechanic. Two cars are matched based on category filters, driver challenge requests, or general community matchmaking. Active community members then evaluate both entries across visual appeal, performance balance, and build uniqueness.

The competitive structure operates on clear parameters designed to ensure fair play across different vehicle styles.

| Phase | Match Duration | Evaluation Criteria | Outcome Determination |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Match Initiation** | Instant / Scheduled | Power-to-weight, build category, or open class | Challenge accepted by opponent |
| **Community Voting** | 12 to 24 Hours | Visual cohesion, authenticity, mod balance | Real-time community upvotes |
| **Round Close** | Automatic | Final vote tallies tallied on public record | Winner gains XP, badge points, and rank |
| **Post-Match Review** | Indefinite | Duel archive accessible on public profiles | Serves as permanent build bragging rights |

To maintain integrity, the system implements voter verification mechanics that discourage multi-account ballot padding. Drivers who consistently participate in fair judging receive voter reputation points, unlocking higher voting weight over time.

### Practical Matchup Tips

* **Select the Right Competitive Bracket:** Do not pit a lightly tuned daily driver against purpose-built drag cars or ultra-rare supercars unless entering novelty open-class matchups.
* **Highlight Hard-to-Find Parts:** If you run rare imported wheels, period-correct interior trim, or custom fabrication, call those details out in your short build description.
* **Keep Images Fresh:** Update your photos after major mod installations. Community members appreciate watching a build evolve across seasons.
* **Stay Active in the Feed:** Drivers who comment thoughtfully on other members’ cards build positive platform recognition that reflects well during voting rounds.

Player experience shows that overstating horsepower figures quickly draws skepticism from experienced automotive enthusiasts. Transparency regarding stock components, ongoing project hurdles, and genuine wheel dyno stats builds far more goodwill among peer judges.
