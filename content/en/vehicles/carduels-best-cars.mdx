For drivers who dislike the sluggish turn rates of the heaviest trucks, muscle and sedan cruisers offer the ideal middle ground. Cruisers retain enough curb weight to withstand glancing blows while boasting superior lateral agility.

## Match Tactics: How to Win with Top-Tier Builds

Even the strongest chassis will lose if you drive recklessly near the arena boundary. Mastering spatial awareness and throttle control elevates your performance from average to lobby leader.

*   **Avoid Head-On Clashes with Equal Weight:** If you spot an opponent driving an identical heavy build, do not charge them straight on from full distance. Instead, angle your vehicle by 15 to 20 degrees right before impact to hit their wheel hub, causing them to fish-tail.

*   **The T-Bone Tactic:** Wait for two rival vehicles to lock bumpers. While they are stationary and fighting for traction, slam into the side of the engagement to score an easy double knockout.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which car has the highest knockback power in Car DUELS?
Vehicles in the Heavy Enforcer archetype equipped with maximum-tier reinforced ram bars deliver the highest knockback force. Their heavy base weight combines with bumper multipliers to send lighter vehicles sliding across the arena.

### Are lightweight cars worth using in competitive matches?
Lightweight cars are viable only for players with exceptional dodging and counter-ramming skills. While fast, they are easily knocked off the edge if caught in a direct collision against heavier builds. Most competitive players prefer balanced cruisers or heavy trucks.

### How do I stop my car from sliding off the edge after a hit?
Equip high-traction tires in the garage to increase surface grip, and prioritize weighted chassis upgrades. If you get shoved toward an edge, let go of the boost immediately, counter-steer toward the center, and tap the reverse throttle to stabilize your vehicle's momentum.

### What is the most cost-effective garage upgrade for beginners?
The most cost-effective early upgrade is a Tier 2 wedge bumper. It costs significantly less than a full chassis swap and grants enough upward leverage to help entry-level cars dislodge and defeat heavier opponents during head-to-head collisions.
